Three-Player Chess

Players

Moves

    Rules
    • White, Red and Black move in turn, counter-clockwise.
    • Pieces move as in normal chess. Lines carry on across the middle into the other players' segments.
    • A diagonal through the very centre continues to the cell directly opposite.
    • Pawns head for the middle and then toward an opponent's back rank. They promote on reaching any opponent's back rank.
    • You can't leave your own king in check. If you're checkmated or have no legal moves, you're out and your pieces leave the board.
    • Moves happen one at a time, so a king can be left exposed to the next player, who may capture it. Losing your king also puts you out.
    • The last player standing wins. Castling works as usual; there's no en passant.
